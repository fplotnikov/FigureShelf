Why it feels calm

Nothing to unlock

No levels, timers, scores or stars. Every figure, color and accessory is there from the first tap.

Gentle by design

Soft colors, slow moves and quiet sounds that only play when you tap. One button turns sound off.

Always reversible

Undo any change. Reset brings the figure back to how it started.

Look as long as you like

Turn a figure around, pinch to zoom right up to an eye, or swipe the menu away to see it full screen.

See it in your room

On your real floor

Tap "See in my room" and the figure stands on your floor through the camera. Tap to move it, pinch to resize, twist to turn.

Still fun without AR

On devices without augmented reality the room button simply hides. The shelf, colors, accessories and moves all work.
