## Description
Figure Shelf is a calm toy box of 3D figures to explore freely. There are no missions, no locks, no scores and no ads. Everything is open from the start.

Pick from 60 figures:
• Animals, from bunnies and kittens to lions, elephants and turtles
• Dinosaurs, realistic and museum-style, from T. rex and Triceratops to a feathered Velociraptor and a gliding Pteranodon
• Imaginary creatures
• Robots that transform into cars, jets, boats and trucks
• Friendly animatronics with clicky robot moves

Make them your own:
• Change colors with one tap
• Add hats, glasses, masks, scarves and more
• Undo any change, or Reset back to the start

Make them move:
• Dance, Jump, Run, Spin and Wave
• Tap Again to keep a move going
• Gentle sounds that play only on a tap, and can be turned off

See them in your room:
• Put any figure on your real floor with the camera
• Tap to move it, pinch to resize, twist to turn

For anyone who likes to look, change and play at their own pace. No accounts, no ads, no tracking and nothing collected.

## App Review notes
No sign-in is needed. Every figure is unlocked. To try augmented reality, tap "See in my room" on an AR-capable device and point the camera at the floor. Swipe the bottom menu down to view a figure full screen, and pinch to zoom. The parents' info (privacy and support links) opens when you press and hold the "i" button for 3 seconds, so young children don't open Safari by accident. The app collects no data and makes no network requests.
